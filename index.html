Last king standing

Chess Royale

No turns. No mercy. Every capture is a secret duel.

How to play
  • Move: click one of your red pieces, then a green square. Pieces move like in chess; pawns step straight and capture diagonally in any direction.
  • No turns: after every move or attack you wait 5 seconds, then go again. So does everyone else.
  • Points are life: King 30, Queen 27, Rook 15, Bishop 9, Knight 9, Pawn 3. Each piece's bar shows what it has left.
  • Attack: click a red square and bid points in secret. An attacked piece can't defend itself: only pieces that protect its square can bid to save it. An unprotected piece falls at once.
  • Defend: when you're attacked, you have 15 seconds to choose what each protecting piece bids. Higher attack wins; a tie holds. Everyone loses what they bid.
  • Firestorm: after 2 minutes the ring of fire closes in every minute. Squares turning red are about to fall into the lava.
  • Lose your king and you're out. Drag to turn the camera, scroll or pinch to zoom.
