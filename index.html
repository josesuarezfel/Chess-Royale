Chess Royale

Free-for-all chess on a round board. There are no turns: after every move you wait a few seconds, then go again. Every capture is a secret bidding duel. Last king standing wins.

You play Red against computer players.

How to play
  • Pieces move like in chess. Pawns step one square straight in any direction and capture one square diagonally in any direction.
  • Points are life: King 30, Queen 27, Rook 15, Bishop 9, Knight 9, Pawn 3.
  • After each move or attack you wait 4 seconds before acting again.
  • Attacking starts a duel. You bid points in secret, the defender bids too and can call one guard covering the square. Every piece loses what it bid; a higher attack wins the square, a tie or higher defense holds.
  • When you're attacked you have 7 seconds to answer, or your piece bids half its points.
  • The board shrinks after 90 seconds and every 45 seconds after that. Red squares are about to fall.
  • Lose your king and you're out. Drag to rotate the camera, scroll or pinch to zoom.
